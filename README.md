# 🚀 Free ATS Resume Scanner

**Stop getting rejected by bots.** This tool compares your resume against a job description to calculate your "ATS Match Score" and highlights the exact keywords you are missing.


## 🧐 Why use this?
Recruiters use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter resumes before a human ever sees them. If your resume lacks specific keywords from the job description, you get auto-rejected.

This tool helps you:
1.  Check your "Match Score" (Aim for 80%+)
2.  Identify **missing hard skills & keywords**
3.  Tailor your resume in seconds, not hours

## 🔒 Privacy & Security (Important)
**Your data is safe.**
This tool runs 100% in your browser (Client-Side).
* ❌ We do not store your resume.
* ❌ We do not send your data to any server.
* ✅ All processing happens locally on your device.

You can verify this by checking the open-source code in `index1.html`.
